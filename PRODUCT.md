# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Short-drama viewers (ReelShort/DramaBox audience) browsing and binge-watching
vertical micro-episodes, on phone (native app + mobile web) and now desktop web.
Also the white-label buyer (a Producer/studio) evaluating the platform as "their
own branded app + website." The consumer is in a lean-back discovery-then-watch
loop; the buyer is judging whether it looks like a real, shippable product.

## Product Purpose

A white-label short-drama streaming platform monetized by a coin economy: browse
a vertical feed, unlock episodes with coins, daily check-in. Success = the
consumer loop feels effortless and premium, and the same codebase renders a
credible phone app AND a credible desktop streaming website per brand.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, editorial, indulgent. Three words: cinematic, editorial, moreish.
Dark theatre with warm gold accents and a Playfair serif voice — closer to a
boutique film magazine than a utilitarian streaming dashboard. The drama is the
hero; chrome recedes.

## Anti-references

- A phone layout stretched or centered in a desktop browser (the current web
  pain — a 430px column on a 1440px screen reads as unfinished).
- Generic SaaS dashboards; cool-gray Netflix-clone chrome with no point of view.
- Cluttered coupon-site density (ReelShort's worst pages). Keep the editorial calm.

## Design Principles

- **The drama is the hero.** Posters and stills carry the page; UI chrome is quiet.
- **One codebase, two registers.** Phone stays phone; desktop becomes a real
  streaming site. Responsive helpers no-op off desktop-web so phones never regress.
- **Earned familiarity on desktop.** Streaming-site conventions users already
  know — top nav, poster grids, hover-to-preview, a cinematic hero.
- **Premium over dense.** Generous spacing and large imagery over coupon-grid clutter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text ≥4.5:1 on the near-black bg. Hover affordances must have a non-hover
fallback (focus states, always-visible titles). Respect prefers-reduced-motion
for hover/scale transitions.
